## What is AbsoluteSite?

**AbsoluteSite** is an NVDA add‑on that gives you instant, one‑press access to all your favorite websites.  
Save, organize, and open any page directly from a single keyboard‑friendly window — no more typing long URLs or digging through bookmarks.

## Key Features

- **Site Manager:** Open all your saved links, neatly arranged in custom categories.
- **Smart Capture:** Double‑tap a hotkey to instantly save the current page you’re browsing.
- **Most Visited:** Automatically tracks the sites you use most and lets you pin favorites.
- **Quick Search:** Type a query and search Google immediately, using any installed browser.
- **Full Control:** Rename, reorder, move, or pin sites. Everything works via keyboard and context menus.
- **Local Storage:** All data is kept in a simple JSON file on your PC — nothing is sent online.

## Why AbsoluteSite?

Screen reader users often spend too much time navigating browser menus just to open a familiar page.  
AbsoluteSite replaces that with a single, predictable keyboard hub — so you can jump straight to what matters.

## How to Use

1. **Open your sites:** Press `Alt+Backspace` once. Choose a category, select a site, and hit Enter.
2. **Save a page:** While browsing, press `Alt+Backspace` twice quickly. The URL is captured automatically; just pick a category and save.
3. **Most Visited:** Press `Alt+Backspace` three times rapidly. This shows your top‑used sites — pin, reorder, or open them instantly.
4. **Search the web:** Press `Windows+Alt+Backspace`. Type your query, select a browser, and press Enter to search Google.

## Hot Keys

| Gesture | Action | Result |
|---------|--------|--------|
| Alt+Backspace (1 tap) | Site Manager | Browse categories and open saved sites |
| Alt+Backspace (2 taps) | Add Current Site | Save the current browser URL to a category |
| Alt+Backspace (3 taps) | Most Visited | View and launch your most‑used websites |
| Windows+Alt+Backspace | Quick Search | Search Google directly from NVDA |

## Data & Privacy

Your sites are stored locally at `%appdata%\nvda\ChaiChaimee\AbsoluteSite\`. Nothing is ever sent online.
